Practice training and evaluating microscopy models. Examine annotation quality, prediction errors and the limits of applying a model to new images.

- Ground truth annotation strategies & quality control
- Model architecture selection (StarDist, Cellpose, U-Net)
- Cloud-based training pipelines (GPU optimization)
- Validation metrics & error analysis
- Running trained models and monitoring their predictions
