A one-day working session for engineering and business leaders accountable for AI adoption outcomes. You write throughout: a leader mandate with measurement attached, a phased adoption plan for your own organization, and the story that travels upward — with honest labels on which numbers are evidence-backed and which are directional.

- Write a leader mandate with real measurement attached — personal, dated, measured
- Pick enablement formats with evidence behind them — and skip the ones buyers usually get wrong
- Draft a phased adoption plan for your organization in the working session, with owners, measures, and kill criteria
- Decide harvest vs. reinvest for capacity the tooling frees up, and write the reinvestment loop into the plan
- Tell the adoption story that travels upward: a 3-minute narrative with one measured result and an honest caveat
