Turn scattered AI experiments into a phased plan with clear leadership commitments and measures of progress.

- Write a personal leadership commitment with a date and a measure of progress.
- Compare training and adoption approaches using organizational cases, then choose an approach for your context.
- Draft a phased plan, decide when to continue or stop and how to use time freed by AI.

Who it is for: Engineering and business leaders responsible for AI adoption.

Schedule: 6 hours, plus breaks and lunch

Preparation and scope: Bring one adoption challenge and a description of what your organization has already tried. Participants write throughout the session and leave with a draft plan to refine and implement in their organization.
