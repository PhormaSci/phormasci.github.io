Build a working tool for your own role — not a toy demo — in guided hackathon sprints. Day 1: guided first ship, prompting as delegation, and the verification habits that separate robust AI-built apps from fragile ones. Day 2: hackathon sprints on your own scoped project, with automated quality gates and a 3-minute demo of what you built.

- Build and demo a working tool for your own role, from idea to running — without writing code by hand
- Apply a verification checklist to any AI-built tool: test with real cases, recognize fragile patterns, state what the tool does not handle
- Run the iterate loop deliberately — describe, generate, verify, refine — instead of prompt-by-prompt luck
- Decide and justify when to stop and call an engineer, with explicit escalation criteria
- Explain your tool to a non-participant in a 3-minute demo story: what it does, where it breaks, what data it touches
