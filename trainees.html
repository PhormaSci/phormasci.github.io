Enterprise AI

AI for Non-Coders

Create a small tool for a recurring task at work, step by step with AI and instructor support.

Create a small tool for a recurring task at work, step by step with AI and instructor support.

  • Start with a tool that summarizes meeting notes, using a workspace prepared for you.
  • Describe a task to the AI, try the tool it creates and check its results with examples.
  • Show what your tool does, explain where it fails and identify when to ask an engineer for help.

Who it is for: Operations, administrative and domain specialists.

Schedule: Day 1: 5 h 45 min; day 2: 6 h, plus breaks and lunch

Preparation and scope: We prepare the workspace and exercises. Use example data unless another data policy is agreed. You will demonstrate a small tool in the workshop; a software professional should review it before it is used in day-to-day work.

Duration: 2 days

Format: Step-by-step practical workshop

Prerequisites: No programming experience required. Bring a recurring task to discuss; guided project options are available.

Outcome: Build and demonstrate a small working tool for one agreed task. Take away a checklist to test it, a description of its limits and guidance on when to seek technical help.

Research with AI

Reproducible Research Code

Help a colleague reproduce a research result without relying on your machine or undocumented setup knowledge.

Help a colleague reproduce a research result without relying on your machine or undocumented setup knowledge.

  • Try to reproduce a result, identify missing setup instructions and record exact software and data versions.
  • Add checks on the results and verify or isolate unchecked changes, including AI-generated changes.
  • Have a colleague reproduce the result and document remaining work with owners and next steps.

Who it is for: Researchers whose code, models or analyses must be reproduced by others.

Schedule: Day 1: 5 h 45 min; day 2: 6 h, plus breaks and lunch

Preparation and scope: Agree on one feasible result and repository before the session. If your code is confidential or too large for the workshop, use sanitized data or the provided practice repository. The reproduction test covers the selected result and documented conditions.

Duration: 2 days

Format: Hands-on workshop

Prerequisites: Ability to run and make basic edits to research scripts. Use a repository scoped before the workshop or a provided practice repository.

Outcome: Enable a colleague to reproduce one selected result from a clean checkout of the workshop repository. Leave with documented environment and data requirements, a passing reproduction test and a prioritized list of remaining verification work.
